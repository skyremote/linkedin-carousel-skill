# NavAIgate worked examples

Ten original sales-sheet images: five topics, each in light and dark. Start with `01_Growth_Partner_light_v1.png` and its dark counterpart to compare hierarchy and contrast. See `walkthrough.txt` for Daniel's account of the exercise.

These images are approximately 1054 × 1492 pixels (one is 1055 × 1491), suitable for digital examples, not 300 dpi A4 print masters. They were generated with the original NavAIgate logo as a reference; generated marks and text are not guaranteed pixel-identical to the source logo or font.

**Known defect:** `02_Commercial_Case_dark_v1.png` has approximate bar proportions despite two correction attempts. Do not copy its chart geometry. `commercial-case-carousel.pdf` uses deterministic 240 px and 80 px bar heights on the same baseline. The 60/20/40-hour numbers are assumptions and targets, not a customer result.

Use these files to study structure and spacing, not as claims for another business. The NavAIgate name and logo remain brand identifiers; publication here does not imply endorsement or permission to impersonate the business. Other organisations mentioned in the examples do not endorse this skill.
